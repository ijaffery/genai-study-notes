# Diffusion Models (DDPM)

*Forward noising process, reverse denoising, score matching, U-Net backbone. Implement a tiny diffusion model from scratch — this is the core of Stable Diffusion.*

## Topics to cover

- Forward noising process (Markov chain)

- U-Net architecture for diffusion
- Training objective derivation
- Sampling from trained models
- Connection to VAEs and score-based models
